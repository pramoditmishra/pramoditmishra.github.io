About me

Current position, background and research focus

I am a Ph.D. scholar in the Department of Mathematics at Mahindra University, Hyderabad, working under the supervision of Dr. Biswarup Biswas. I began the programme in January 2026, after working as a Junior Research Fellow on a SERB-sponsored project in the same department.

My work centres on the design, analysis and implementation of robust, high-order computational schemes for relativistic hydrodynamic flows. I completed my M.Sc. in Mathematics at the University of Allahabad, Prayagraj.

Research interests

  • Numerical Analysis
  • Hyperbolic PDEs
  • Relativistic Astrophysical Fluid Flows
  • Modern Stable Numerical Schemes
